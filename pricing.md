# Pricing — Eye Rest

Last updated: 2026-08-16

Eye Rest is a native macOS (14+) menu-bar app for the 20-20-20 eye-rest rule. Freemium, one-time unlock, no subscription.

## Free
- Price: $0
- Platform: macOS 14 or later
- Included: 20-20-20 menu-bar timer, full-screen hand-painted break, one time-of-day scene, pre-break heads-up (skip or snooze any break), blink and posture nudges
- Not included: the other five scenes + scene picker, smart auto-pause, office-hours schedule, stats

## Pro
- Price: $14.99 one-time (no subscription, no recurring charge)
- Trial: 14-day full-feature trial, no card required
- Devices: up to 3 Macs per license (launch offer)
- Included: everything in Free, plus all six hand-painted scenes + picker, living ambient animation, smart auto-pause (video calls, browser video, idle), office-hours schedule, stats and streaks
