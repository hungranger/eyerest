Getting started

How do I install Eye Rest?

Download the app, drag it to your Applications folder, and open it. Eye Rest lives in your menu bar — look for the small icon at the top-right of your screen. Your 14-day Pro trial starts automatically.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

Where did the app go? I don't see a window.

Eye Rest is a menu-bar app — it has no dock icon or main window by design. Click its icon in the menu bar (top-right) to see the countdown, take a break, or open Settings.

Licenses & Pro

How do I unlock Pro?

Buy a license key, then open Eye Rest → Settings → Unlock Pro and paste your key. Pro unlocks all six scenes, living animation, smart pause, office hours, nudges, and stats. It's a one-time $14.99 — no subscription.

I bought a key but it won't activate.

Make sure you copied the whole key with no extra spaces, and that you're connected to the internet (activation checks the key once with the seller). Still stuck? Email us with your purchase email and we'll sort it out.

Can I use my license on more than one Mac?

Yes — activate it on your Macs the same way (Settings → Unlock Pro).

Do I keep Pro if I reinstall?

Yes. Just paste your key again after reinstalling.

Using Eye Rest

It interrupts me during meetings.

Turn on Smart Pause in Settings — it skips breaks while your camera or microphone is in use (a meeting) or a video is playing. You can also set Office Hours so it only runs during your workday.

How do I change how often breaks happen?

Open Settings from the menu-bar icon to adjust the schedule and which scenes appear.
