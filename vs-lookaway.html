Where LookAway wins

Say it plainly: LookAway is a genuinely good app. If you want the deepest, most configurable break tool, it is the safer pick today. It has more years of refinement, more granular scheduling and wellness controls, and a well-developed smart-pause system. If your priority is breadth of settings and a proven track record, LookAway earns it.

Where Eye Rest is different

Eye Rest makes one bet: you take the break you want to look at. Instead of dimming, your screen fades into a hand-painted, time-of-day scene with gentle motion, fireflies at dusk, god-rays through leaves, rain on glass. The point is not "prettier." It is adherence. The most common reason people quit break apps is that the interruption is unpleasant, so they turn it off and go back to sore eyes. A break you look forward to is one you keep.

It is also simpler to buy: one flat $14.99, one time, up to 3 Macs, no per-device math and no subscription.

Price, specifically

  • Eye Rest: $14.99 one-time. 14-day full trial. One license covers up to 3 Macs (launch offer). No subscription.
  • LookAway: per-device tiers (single device at the low end, more for multi-device), with a lifetime option. Check LookAway's site for current numbers before you decide.

If you want a single price with no tier decision, Eye Rest is the cleaner call. If you need a specific device count LookAway packages well, price it out.

Pick LookAway if

  • You want the most mature, most configurable break app available today.
  • Depth of wellness controls and a long track record matter more than the look of the break.

Pick Eye Rest if

  • You have uninstalled break apps before because the interruption was annoying.
  • You want the break to be a calm, beautiful moment, not a dimmed screen.
  • You prefer one flat one-time price over device tiers.
