How it works

The 20-20-20 rule is the standard fix for digital eye strain: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for 20 seconds, so your eyes' focusing muscle gets a rest. This timer just keeps count for you. Press start, work as normal, and when the break screen appears, look out a window or across the room until it counts down.
